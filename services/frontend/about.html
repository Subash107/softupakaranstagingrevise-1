Our Story

Built because buying digital products in Nepal was broken

Grey-market sellers. Unreliable delivery. No local payment support. That was the reality for anyone trying to buy a Netflix subscription or a WordPress plugin in Nepal. We started SoftUpakaran to fix that — a single, trusted place where Nepali customers get exactly what they pay for, instantly, at fair prices.

Every order is confirmed within minutes via WhatsApp. Every payment goes through eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay or bank transfer. No grey market, no waiting days for a reply.
