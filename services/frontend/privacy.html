Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use SoftUpakaran, we may collect:

  • Order information — name, contact (WhatsApp/email), and items ordered.
  • Payment confirmation — screenshot or transaction reference you send us; we do not store card or wallet credentials.
  • Usage data — pages visited, browser type, and device type via standard server logs.
  • Locally stored preferences — theme, language, cart contents, and wishlist stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill and confirm your orders via WhatsApp.
  • To provide customer support and handle refund requests.
  • To improve the site based on aggregate usage patterns.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your cart, wishlist, language preference, and theme. No cross-site tracking cookies are used. Google Sign-In (optional) uses Google's own authentication flow; we only receive a user token, not your Google password.

4. Data Security

All communication between your browser and our servers uses HTTPS encryption. Payment transactions are handled directly by eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, or your bank — SoftUpakaran never processes raw payment credentials.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any personal data we hold by contacting us on WhatsApp or email. Since most data is stored locally in your browser, you can also clear it at any time via your browser settings.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, reach us via WhatsApp.
